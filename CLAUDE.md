# CLAUDE.md

- Do not download any model locally


## What is explicitly excluded?
1. Autonomous enforcement
The system will not independently suspend sellers, delist products, freeze payments, or impose penalties. Humans retain decision authority.

2. A complete marketplace-wide rollout
The pilot will not cover all five business units, every seller category, every case type, or every geography.

3. Replacement of existing enterprise systems
We will not rebuild Jira, the marketplace's seller portal, its order-management system, or its core compliance platform.

4. Unapproved integrations or data access
No unrestricted production-database access, arbitrary external actions, or integrations without customer authorisation.

5. Guaranteed accuracy or business results
We will not guarantee 70% faster turnaround, zero errors, perfect policy interpretation, or a particular financial saving before measurement.

6. A general-purpose AI assistant
No unrelated customer support chatbot, seller-facing chatbot, general sales agent, or broad enterprise automation platform.

7. Full policy and legal decision-making
The platform will not invent policies, replace the legal team, or independently decide how ambiguous regulations should be interpreted.
