# SCOPING CYCLE

## What exactly are we building or delivering?
We are building an internal AI-assisted case-handling system for e-commerce marketplace compliance teams.
It helps employees investigate seller-compliance cases, gather policy evidence, coordinate specialist analysis, and prepare consistent recommendations—with human approval before consequential actions.
The initial product is a single-workflow pilot, not a full marketplace-wide AI platform.
